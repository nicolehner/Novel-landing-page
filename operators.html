Why it's legal

California classifies by process, not by taste.

Under California law, what separates a fermented beverage from a distilled spirit is how the alcohol is produced — not what the finished drink tastes like. Distillation makes a spirit. Fermentation does not.

Every Novel cocktail is built on a fermented base with natural flavors. Nothing in the keg has ever touched a still. That keeps every pour squarely inside what your Type 41 or Type 42 license already authorizes you to sell.

What that means at the bar

Cocktail revenue without the Type 47.

A full-liquor license upgrade can cost six figures on the open market — when one is even available in your county. Novel skips the entire problem: you pour cocktail-strength drinks at 15.9% ABV, priced like cocktails, on the license you already hold.
