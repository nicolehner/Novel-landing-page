For operators

Yes. California distinguishes fermented beverages from distilled spirits by production method — fermentation versus distillation — not by flavor or format. Novel is fermented with natural flavors and never distilled, so it falls within what your beer & wine license already authorizes. We provide compliance documentation to every account during onboarding.
